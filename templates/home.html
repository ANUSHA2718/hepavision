Capabilities

A complete CT-to-report diagnostic pipeline

Every stage of the workflow — from raw CT slice to a downloadable diagnostic report — runs on the trained TensorFlow models integrated into the Flask backend.

Liver Segmentation

U-Net based liver isolation from abdominal CT slices, delivering pixel-level hepatic boundaries.

Tumor Segmentation

A second U-Net localizes lesion regions constrained within the predicted liver mask.

Quantitative Metrics

Tumor burden, pixel counts, and lesion count computed directly from model outputs.

Instant PDF Report

A premium diagnostic report is generated automatically after every analysis.
